Flask of Rainbow Fountain Water

Can be used to morph your neopet into a random available color for their species, including alternate colors.

Carbonated Rainbow Fountain Water™️

Can be used to morph your neopet into a random available color for their species Excluding alternate and basic colors e.g. Green, Blue, Yellow, Red, Purple, Silver, Gold etc.

Mysterious Swirly Potion

Can be used to morph your neopet into a random Limited Edition species (Chomby, Cybunny, Hissi, Jetsam, Kiko, Koi, Poogle, and Tonu), retaining their current color if available in the species they morph into.

Chaos Jar

Can be used to morph your neopet into a random species and color combination, no exclusions or limitations. The ultimate gamble.

Scary Dark Potion

-10 LVL, -10 STR, +10 HP. Useful when you have excess LVL and STR.

Flask of Clear, Odourless Liquid

+1-3 of a random stat, makes your neopet sick with Grumbles.

Alternatively, allows you to draw two addional cards in a Yu-Gi-Oh duel.
Genius Negg

+1-3 INT and a random book.

Icestorm Negg

Chance to change your neopet's color to Ice.

Faerie Queen Negg

+1 HP and may also give a faerie quest or change your pet's color to Faerie.

Rainwater Negg

+1 DEF and 25% chance of receiving a Flask of Rainbow. Fountain Water

Cracked Negg

Gives a random unpainted petpet of any rarity.

Bloatershroom

Changes your neopet's hunger from Bloated to Very Full. Cannot be fed when your neopet is already Very Bloated.

Yellow Healthshroom

Cures any disease or illness.

Spindly Mushrooms

-1 LVL. Useful when your pet overlevels their training bracket.

Poké Ball

When thrown, give you a random painted petpet, no restrictions or exclusions.

Monotonous Dial

Once used, you permanently gain the ability to receive a notification when the Wheel of Monotony is about to stop spinning.

Game Controller

Can be mixed in the Cooking Pot with a World Challenge Map piece to get a different piece from the same set. e.g. Meridell 3.1 > Meridell 3.14

Green Candy

+1-2 HP.

Blue Candy

+1-2 DEF.

Red Candy

+1-2 STR.

White Candy

+1-2 AGI.

Something Mysterious

Grants a random item from your wishlist. Useful for removing all but one item from your wishlist to get something specific. Some limitations apply e.g. no Super Attack Pea. Full limitations unknown.

Something Very Mysterious

Difference from Something Mysterious Unknown. Grants a random item from your wishlist. Useful for removing all but one item from your wishlist to get something specific. Some limitations apply i.e. no Super Attack Pea. Full limitations unknown.

Choco Spray

WARNING, NEEDS TO BE USED NOT FED.
Can be used to turn your neopet chocolate.

Choco Spritz

Can be fed to a petpet via the petpet interaction page to turn them chocolate.

Strange Potion

Using this potion on your pet will turn them Purple, if it is available for their species.

Sparkle Potion

Using this potion on your pet will allow you to choose a new gender for them.
